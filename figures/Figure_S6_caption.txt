Figure S6. Original versus corrected pre-grasp trajectory (handover_block).
The original executor sent only samples 9, 19, 29, 39 and 49 of the 50-sample pre-grasp chunk to the robot, so the chunk's standoff point (sample 34) was never commanded: the end effector crossed from the off-axis sample 29 (median 4.0 cm lateral to the final approach axis) diagonally to sample 39 and then to the grasp target, cutting the corner (A, B). The corrected executor commands samples 9, 19, 29, 34, 39, 44, 49 through an aligned lead-in point, the standoff point and a straight final segment along the gripper finger axis; the end effector is on the approach axis from sample 29 onward (median lateral offset 0.005 cm at sample 29) and its commanded-versus-measured error is checked at the standoff. Decoder, grounding and checkpoints are unchanged.
(A, B) Measured end-effector positions after each executed sample in a top-down plane rotated so that the final approach (standoff to target G) points along +x with G at the origin; thin lines are all n = 114 matched development episodes per executor, bold lines a representative episode; positions between executed samples were not logged and are connected by straight lines. The original standoff is reconstructed from the logged StagePlan variables (G minus pre-grasp distance times approach direction); the corrected one is the commanded sample-34 pose.
(A, B, D, E) DEVELOPMENT COMPARISON on matched seeds: development blocks D/E/F, Clean and Randomized pooled, restricted to seeds present in both runs (n = 114 of 120). (C) Dev groups: matched development seeds, original vs corrected, Clean 36/58 vs 51/58 and Randomized 17/56 vs 29/56. Hatched "Final" groups: frozen final evaluation on fresh seeds (Clean 8200000+, Randomized 8700000+, n = 20), previous frozen configuration vs corrected configuration: Clean 14/20 vs 15/20 and Randomized 6/20 vs 9/20; these are a different seed set, not matched to the development bars, and for Randomized only 18 seeds are shared. Error bars: Wilson 95% intervals.
(D, E) First grasp of the first cycle: the held fraction by grounding error of the grasp point (privileged object pose, analysis only; never a policy input), and outcome categories. Among first grasps with grounding error <= 1.5 cm, 58/70 were held with the original and 71/73 with the corrected executor (D); missed grasps with grounding error <= 1.5 cm fell from 12 to 2 (execution/alignment) in E, while misses with grounding error > 1.5 cm (perception) changed little (35 to 30). First grasps held rose from 67/114 to 82/114.
Conclusion: correcting the execution path of the unchanged V21 pre-grasp chunk removed the corner cutting, improved the quality of the pre-grasp approach and the first-grasp hold rate, and raised matched-seed development success; the remaining failures are dominated by grounding errors above about 1.5 cm. Final-run differences at n = 20 are within overlapping intervals.
