## 4. What was analysed, what was changed, what was rejected (narrative; numbers are in the tables above and in the CSV files)

### 4.1 Where the grounding-to-action discrepancy comes from (development seeds, privileged object poses used for offline analysis only)
* **Execution / IK / controller: not the problem.** Commanded versus measured end-effector pose differs by about 0.0 cm at every closure (`exec` column of `scripts/analysis/clc_diag.py`). No coordinate-transform error was found: the head-camera ray passes 0.25-0.6 cm from the object axis.
* **Decoding / chunk sampling: the main execution defect.** The pre-grasp chunk of the unchanged StagePlan executor has 50 samples (waypoint, standoff point at sample 34, grasp point at sample 49) but the executor sent only every 10th sample (9, 19, 29, 39, 49). The standoff point was never visited, so the gripper travelled diagonally from the waypoint into the object. Measured consequence for handover_block (frozen configuration, 60 Randomized development episodes): the box had already been pushed by more than 1 cm before the fingers closed in 30 of 60 first grasps, and the median gripper-to-box error at closure was 4.8 cm although the median grounding error was 2.1 cm.
* **Perception: the remaining limit.** The lateral component of the triangulated grasp point comes from the wrist-camera proposal peak (about 1 cm in Randomized scenes, 0.45 cm in Clean scenes); in cluttered scenes a wrong wrist peak is associated or no second view succeeds and the coarse fallback is used. For place_container_plate the dominant Randomized failure is selection of the container in the head view (wrong, unconfident or missing proposals), followed by slips and placement misses.

### 4.2 What was changed (all inside the existing executor; no new network, no retraining, every checkpoint bit-identical to freeze3)
`scripts/clc_rt_policy.py` is a versioned copy of the frozen `pic_rt_policy.py`; every option is off by default, so the frozen behaviour is reproduced without options.
1. **Aligned approach with standoff verification** (`CLC_APPROACH=2`; handover_block and place_container_plate). The same decoded pre-grasp chunk is executed through an aligned lead-in point, the standoff point and a straight final segment along the gripper finger axis (samples 9, 19, 29, 34, 39, 44, 49). At the standoff the commanded pose is compared with the measured pose and the step is repeated once if they differ by more than 1 cm. K = 5 is unchanged for every other decoder call; only the pre-grasp chunk is sampled more densely near the critical event (two extra calls per grasp).
2. **Placement verification with bounded retry** (`CLC_REVERIFY=1`; place_container_plate). After release the arm lifts, the last observation view is re-grounded with the unchanged proposal heads and referent scorer, and if the container is confidently seen more than 10 cm from where the gripper released it the stage is retried (bounded by the existing limit of two retries). The retry re-grounds the same instructed referent, so it cannot change the intended object.
3. **Grounding cross-checks for the single-arm path** (`ACL_NEWG=1`; place_container_plate): the confidence gate and low-threshold re-proposal that the two-arm tasks already used.
4. **Grounded fallback** (`CLC_FB=1`; place_container_plate): when two-view grounding fails but the head referent is confident, the grasp target is the coarse head estimate plus the mean grasp offset measured on training-domain rollouts (`clc_stats.json`), instead of the StagePlan regression.

### 4.3 Tested and rejected on development evidence
* Close-range lateral correction from near wrist views (`CLC_NEAR`): it reduces the median grounding error of the handover first grasp (Randomized 1.75 -> 1.15 cm) but not task success (31/60 vs 32/60 Randomized, 50/60 vs 53/60 Clean) and costs 8 more calls per episode. The first version, without a return to a safe pose, was harmful (6/20, 13/20).
* Prior-consistent wrist association with re-centred views (`CLC_ASSOC`, `CLC_RECENTER`): no gain in grounding accuracy; Clean 16/20 vs 19/20.
* Standoff sample alone without the aligned lead-in (`CLC_APPROACH=1`): 9/20 and 15/20 vs 11/20 and 19/20.
* The aligned approach for stack_blocks_two: 45/60 vs 37/60 Randomized, but the gain comes from one of three seed blocks (+1, -2, +9) and Clean is unchanged (55/60 both). The successful frozen configuration is kept, as the task statement asks.
* Not repeated because they were rejected in the previous study: gripper-yaw hypothesis, StagePlan rotation refresh, 3-D receive pivot, top-down refinement, retrained AnchorHead, refreshed proposal heads / reachability-aware views for place_container_plate.

### 4.4 Language consistency
The instruction string is re-encoded with every refreshed observation exactly as before; the correction steps only re-ground with the same referent scorer and the same instruction. stack_blocks_two keeps the stage-conditioned subgoal scorer and the instruction-conditioned progress head of freeze3 unchanged (identical hashes, see `freeze4/MANIFEST_FREEZE4.sha256`), so block identity and stacking order are handled exactly as in the previous frozen model. No correction path selects a referent by any rule other than the existing instruction-conditioned scorer.

### 4.5 Limitations
* n = 20 per final cell; a difference of 1-3 episodes in a single cell is noise (identical configurations on identical seeds differ by this much, see the click_bell and stack_blocks_two rows, which are two runs of the same frozen configuration). The development evidence (n = 60 per cell, three seed blocks) is the stronger basis for the selection.
* handover_block Randomized: when the first grounding error exceeds about 1.5 cm the grasp still fails and a toppled box is not recovered by the retries. This is a perception limit of the wrist proposal heads in cluttered scenes; it was not solved.
* place_container_plate Randomized: the head-view referent selection still fails in a substantial fraction of scenes; the grounded fallback succeeds in only about half of its uses, which is no better than the StagePlan fallback it replaces when counted per use. The measured gain of the selected setting comes from the combination: episodes whose first attempt was fully grounded succeed in 23 of 26 instead of 13 of 25, and verified retries end in success in 7 of 16 retried episodes (Randomized development episodes, n = 60). The individual contributions were not separated by an exhaustive ablation.
* The verification step cannot decide in a large share of episodes (no confident referent after release); in those cases the executor proceeds without retry.
* Privileged simulator states were used only for the offline diagnostics above (trace option, development runs). The final runs were made without the trace option.
* `freeze/MANIFEST_FREEZE.sha256` (first freeze) lists four training / data-generation scripts that were edited in earlier studies after that freeze; no frozen model, result file or inference source changed. `freeze2` and `freeze3` verify completely.
